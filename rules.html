Brick City RP Rules

Welcome to the official Brick City RP rulebook. These rules are designed to maintain a serious, realistic, and enjoyable roleplay environment for every member of the community. By participating in the server, you agree to follow all listed rules and understand that staff may issue punishments at their discretion when necessary.

General Rules

Age Requirement

You must meet the server’s minimum age requirement in order to maintain access to Brick City RP. Any exceptions are handled directly by management and are never guaranteed. Attempting to lie about your age may result in immediate removal from the community.

Conduct & Respect

Respect is expected at all times, both in-character and out-of-character. Harassment, discrimination, excessive toxicity, or targeted behavior toward other players will not be tolerated. Keep interactions mature and respectful regardless of the situation.

Staff Authority

Staff have final authority over all server situations. If a staff member instructs you to stop something or cooperate with an investigation, you are expected to comply immediately. Disrespecting or obstructing staff may result in removal from the server.

Metagaming & External Information

Using information gained from Discord calls, livestreams, chats, or any outside source for in-character decisions is strictly prohibited. If your character did not learn something through roleplay, your character cannot act on that information.

Punishment: Warn → Warn + Kick → 7 Day Ban

Ban Evasion

Attempting to bypass punishments through alternate accounts, VPNs, spoofers, or any other method is strictly prohibited and may result in a permanent removal from the community.

Punishment: Permanent Ban

Reporting Requirements

Submitting Reports

All reports must contain clear and unedited video evidence that accurately represents the full situation. Clips should include proper context and clearly identify every involved player. A minimum of three minutes is expected unless the situation specifically requires additional footage.

Reports must be submitted within 24 hours of the incident. Delayed reports or reports lacking evidence may be denied without review. Weaponizing the report system, filing false reports, or creating reports out of anger or revenge may result in punishment.

Tebex Item Loss

Tebex Guidelines

Tebex items remain subject to roleplay at all times. If an item is lost through legitimate in-character interaction, it will not be refunded. Claims regarding lost items require a minimum three-minute clip clearly showing the full situation and any associated rule breaks.

Attempting to use purchased items or perks to avoid roleplay, bypass server mechanics, or gain unfair advantages may result in punishment or permanent removal of purchased content.

Roleplay Rules

FailRP

Roleplay should remain realistic, immersive, and believable at all times. Ignoring injuries, forcing unrealistic outcomes, refusing to acknowledge consequences, or behaving in ways that destroy immersion may be considered FailRP.

Punishment: Warn → Warn → 5 Day Ban

FearRP

Players are expected to value their character’s life realistically. Ignoring threats while outnumbered, refusing reasonable demands while at gunpoint, or acting recklessly in dangerous situations may be considered FearRP.

Punishment: Warn → Warn → 3 Day Ban

Powergaming

Powergaming refers to performing unrealistic actions or forcing unrealistic outcomes onto another player. Roleplay should always allow reasonable reactions and remain grounded within the server’s realistic environment.

Punishment: Warn → Warn → 3 Day Ban

Green Zone Conduct

Green Zones

Green Zones are considered safe public areas. Crime, hostility, shootings, kidnapping, and violent confrontations are not allowed inside these locations. Players may not drag active situations into Green Zones in order to avoid consequences or force protection.

Punishment: Warn → Warn + Kick → 5 Day Ban

Interaction & Initiation Guidelines

Initiation

Hostile interactions require proper verbal initiation and roleplay buildup before violence may occur. Players may not immediately escalate situations without giving others a reasonable chance to react or comply.

Third Partying

Joining an active shootout or criminal situation without prior involvement or proper roleplay context is prohibited.

Punishment: Warn → Warn + Kick → 5 Day Ban

Criminal Rules

Kill or Rob

Players may either kill another player or rob them during an interaction, but not both. If a player has already been fully downed, they may not also be robbed afterward.

Hostages

Hostages must be taken through force and proper roleplay. Asking somebody to voluntarily become a hostage is not considered valid roleplay. Demands should remain realistic and balanced.

Combat Logging

Disconnecting during active roleplay to avoid consequences, death, police interaction, or administrative situations is prohibited.

Punishment: 3 Day Ban → 7 Day Ban

VDM

Using vehicles as weapons without valid roleplay reason is prohibited. Vehicles should not be intentionally used to kill or severely injure players outside of realistic circumstances.

Punishment: Warn → Vehicle Blacklist/Kick → 5 Day Ban

EMS Rules

Medical Roleplay

EMS personnel are expected to prioritize patient care and maintain professional, realistic medical roleplay at all times. Unrealistic treatment, rushing scenes, or participating in criminal activity while employed as EMS is prohibited.

PD Rules

Law Enforcement Expectations

Police officers are expected to remain professional and follow proper procedures during all interactions. Abuse of power, corruption without approval, targeting players unfairly, or developing a “win mentality” will not be tolerated.

Weazel News

Media Conduct

Weazel News employees must remain neutral and professional while reporting. False reporting, interfering in active scenes without permission, or using media access for personal gain is prohibited.

Staff Rules

Staff Conduct

Staff members are expected to remain professional, respectful, and unbiased at all times. Abuse of commands, favoritism, leaking sensitive information, or using staff tools for roleplay gain is strictly prohibited.

Staff should behave as though they are using two separate accounts — one for moderation and one for roleplay. The two should never overlap.

Administrative Expectations

Moderators are expected to investigate reports fairly, gather proper evidence before issuing punishments, and remain calm during all staff interactions. Toxicity within the staff team will not be tolerated.

Staff Roles

Trial Moderator

Trial Moderators are community members undergoing evaluation before receiving full staff permissions. They are expected to remain active, helpful, and professional while assisting the staff team.

Moderator

Moderators are responsible for investigating reports, collecting evidence, issuing punishments, and resolving player disputes fairly and professionally.

Administrator

Administrators possess advanced permissions and technical access to the server. They are responsible for community management, server maintenance, and resolving major issues or disputes.

Staff Punishments

Strike System

Staff warnings remain permanently attached to a staff member’s record. Repeated issues or serious violations may result in strikes, suspension, demotion, or permanent termination depending on severity.

Punishment Guidelines

RDM (Random Deathmatch)

Killing another player without valid roleplay reason.

Warn → Warn + Kick → 1 Week Ban

NITRP (No Intent To Roleplay)

Joining the server only to troll, disrupt roleplay, or intentionally ruin scenes rather than participate seriously.

1 Month Ban

Stream Sniping

Using livestreams or external content to locate players or gain information in-game.

14 Day Ban

Doxing / Real Life Threats

Sharing personal information, threatening physical harm, or attempting to intimidate players outside the game environment.

Permanent Ban

Definitions

RDM

Random Deathmatch — killing another player without valid roleplay reason.

VDM

Vehicle Deathmatch — using vehicles as weapons without valid roleplay reason.

Metagaming

Using out-of-character information in-character.

Powergaming

Performing unrealistic actions or forcing actions unfairly onto other players.

NITRP

No Intent To Roleplay — joining only to troll or intentionally disrupt roleplay.
