Brick City Roleplay Documentation

This documentation outlines all official rules, expectations, and systems within Brick City RP. Every player is expected to understand and follow these rules to maintain a realistic and fair roleplay environment.

General Rules

Respect & Conduct

All players must remain respectful at all times. Toxicity, harassment, discrimination, or attempts to ruin another player’s experience are strictly prohibited.

Server Integrity

Exploiting bugs, glitches, or unintended mechanics is not allowed. Any discovered exploits must be reported immediately.

Realism Standard

All roleplay must follow realistic logic. Unrealistic actions that break immersion may be classified as FailRP.

New Life Rule

Definition

When your character dies, they lose memory of the events leading up to their death. You may not return to the area of death or continue related roleplay.

Strict Enforcement

Metagaming

Rule

Using information from Discord, streams, or external sources in-character is prohibited. All knowledge must be gained through roleplay.

Warn → Ban

FailRP

Definition

FailRP includes any unrealistic action that breaks immersion such as ignoring injuries, unrealistic survival, or refusing consequences.

Punishable Offense

FearRP

Behavior Expectation

You must value your character’s life. Acting recklessly under threat of death or ignoring weapons pointed at you is not allowed.

Combat Rules

Initiation Requirement

All hostile actions require clear roleplay initiation before engagement.

Third Partying

Joining active gunfights without context or roleplay reason is prohibited.

Criminal Roleplay

Hostage Rules

Hostages must be taken through valid force and realistic roleplay. Voluntary hostages are not valid.

Robbery Rules

You may not kill and fully rob the same player in a single interaction.

Green Zones

Safe Areas

Green Zones are protected areas where violence, robbery, and kidnapping are strictly prohibited.

Police Department

Conduct

Officers must remain professional, realistic, and unbiased at all times.

EMS

Medical RP

EMS must provide realistic medical care and may not participate in criminal activity while on duty.

Weazel News

Journalism Rules

Weazel News must remain neutral and cannot interfere with active roleplay scenes.

Staff Rules

Expectations

Staff must remain unbiased, professional, and never use administrative tools for personal gain.

Punishment System

RDM

Random Deathmatch — killing without valid roleplay reason.

Warn → Ban

VDM

Vehicle Deathmatch — using vehicles to harm players unrealistically.

Warn → Ban

NITRP

No Intent To Roleplay — joining only to troll or disrupt roleplay.

Permanent Ban Possible
